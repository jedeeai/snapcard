# SnapCard Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**English**

SnapCard does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

- All processing happens locally in your browser.
- The extension reads only the content of posts you are already viewing on x.com, and only when you click the card button.
- Your preferences and uploaded background images are stored locally via Chrome storage and never leave your device.
- When you explicitly enable the translation feature, the text of the selected post is sent to Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com) to obtain a translation. No other data is sent to any server.
- SnapCard contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Contact: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jedeeai/snapcard/issues)

**中文**

SnapCard 不收集、不存储、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。

- 所有处理都在你的浏览器本地完成。
- 插件只在你点击「生成卡片」按钮时，读取你当前正在浏览的那条推文内容。
- 你的偏好设置和上传的背景图都存在浏览器本地，不会离开你的设备。
- 只有当你主动打开「翻译」开关时，那条推文的文本才会发送给谷歌翻译（translate.googleapis.com）以获取译文。除此之外没有任何数据发往任何服务器。
- 插件不含任何统计、追踪或广告代码。

联系方式：[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jedeeai/snapcard/issues)
